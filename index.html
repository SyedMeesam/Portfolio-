Seven services. One person accountable for all of them. No retainer padding, no forty-page audit nobody reads — just the work that moves a number you actually care about.

↗
01AI SEO & GEO+

When somebody asks ChatGPT, Perplexity or Google's AI Overviews who to hire, your name should be the answer. I build the entity signals, structured data and citation-worthy content that gets you named — not just ranked. This is the one everybody talks about and almost nobody can actually do.

02SEO Strategy+

Crawl budget, index bloat, Core Web Vitals, schema, internal linking. Unsexy work, enormous consequences. This is the part that decides whether your best content is ever seen at all, and it is where most sites quietly lose.

03Local SEO+

If you serve one city, you do not need to rank worldwide — you need to own the map pack. Google Business Profile, reviews, local citations, service-area pages. The Texas plumber I worked with pulled 69.1K impressions in three months. In one city.

04E-commerce SEO+

Category architecture, faceted navigation, product schema, and the unglamorous crawl discipline that lets ten thousand SKUs get found. PC-Ricondizionati is 757K impressions in sixteen months. Comfort Style is 16.7K clicks in twelve.

05Web Design+

WordPress and Shopify sites built around one question: what makes a stranger click, trust and buy? Fast, accessible, and handed over properly — not held hostage on a retainer that never ends.

06Google Ads+

Search, Shopping and Performance Max, judged on cost per conversion instead of clicks. Shopping runs at €3.83 per conversion on the Italy account. That is the standard, not the story.

07Meta Ads+

Facebook and Instagram campaigns built for margin. Creative, audiences, retargeting and the boring weekly pruning most accounts never get. B2B leads on the Norway account land at €1.68.
