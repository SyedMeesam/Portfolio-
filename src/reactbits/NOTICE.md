# React Bits components

`Magnet.jsx`, `GradientText.jsx`, `GradientText.css`, `CountUp.jsx`,
`GradientASCII.jsx`, `GradientASCII.css`, `Grainient.jsx`, `Grainient.css`, and
the interface-sound engine in `src/sound/` (`react-bits-sound.js` and
`src/sound/recipes/*.js`, from the React Bits Sound Lab) are from
[React Bits](https://github.com/DavidHDev/react-bits) by David Haz. They are
included as part of this website under the terms in `LICENSE.md` in this folder.

`SplashCursor` was removed in favour of the `Grainient` backdrop.
